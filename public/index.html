Field guide

Ranger · Accurate long-range shots, including flyers.

Snowmaker · Magic snowballs slow, freeze, and deal 35% bonus damage to shielded guards.

Beehive · Low-damage swarms launch at every enemy in range and follow their targets.

Cougar Cave · Football defenders block ground enemies at a rally point.

Enemy signals · Silver-edged shields mean armor. Ice outlines mean frozen; blue rings mean slowed; BLOCKED means defenders are holding the enemy.

Enemies reaching the valley cost one life; the mountain giant costs five. Survive eight waves to win. Shield-bearing rock guards take 45% less damage. Snowmakers bypass armor with 35% bonus damage; tier 4 Sharpshooters also bypass armor. Normal enemies have no shield.

Build and upgrade at any time. Between waves, take as long as you need.
