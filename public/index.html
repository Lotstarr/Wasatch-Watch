Living stone · Copper splitters break into two fast shards when defeated. Layer slowing and swarm damage at the bends.

Targeting · Select a Ranger or Snowmaker to prioritize First, Strongest, Flyers, Healers, or Armored. Beehives always swarm every enemy in range.

Enemy signals · Silver-edged shields mean armor. Ice outlines mean frozen; blue rings mean slowed; HELD means defenders are holding the enemy.

Enemies reaching the valley cost one life; the mountain giant costs five. Survive eight waves to win. Shield-bearing rock guards take 45% less damage. Snowmakers bypass armor with 35% bonus damage; tier 4 Sharpshooters also bypass armor. Normal enemies have no shield.

Build and upgrade at any time. Between waves, take as long as you need.
